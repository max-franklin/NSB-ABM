\section*{Feed-Forward Neural Network Representation}

Let the input vector \( X \) be a \( 1 \times 21 \) vector:
\[
X = \begin{bmatrix}
x_1 & x_2 & \dots & x_{21}
\end{bmatrix}
\]

### Step 1: Input to Hidden Layer

Let the weight matrix \( W_1 \) between the input layer and the hidden layer be of size \( 21 \times 6 \):
\[
W_1 = \begin{bmatrix}
w_{11}^{(1)} & w_{12}^{(1)} & \dots & w_{16}^{(1)} \\
w_{21}^{(1)} & w_{22}^{(1)} & \dots & w_{26}^{(1)} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
w_{211}^{(1)} & w_{212}^{(1)} & \dots & w_{216}^{(1)}
\end{bmatrix}
\]

The bias vector \( b_1 \) for the hidden layer is \( 1 \times 6 \):
\[
b_1 = \begin{bmatrix}
b_{11} & b_{12} & \dots & b_{16}
\end{bmatrix}
\]

The output of the hidden layer \( H \) is calculated as:
\[
H = X W_1 + b_1 = \begin{bmatrix}
h_1 & h_2 & \dots & h_6
\end{bmatrix}
\]

### Step 2: Hidden Layer to Output Layer

Let the weight matrix \( W_2 \) between the hidden layer and the output layer be of size \( 6 \times 5 \):
\[
W_2 = \begin{bmatrix}
w_{11}^{(2)} & w_{12}^{(2)} & \dots & w_{15}^{(2)} \\
w_{21}^{(2)} & w_{22}^{(2)} & \dots & w_{25}^{(2)} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
w_{61}^{(2)} & w_{62}^{(2)} & \dots & w_{65}^{(2)}
\end{bmatrix}
\]

The bias vector \( b_2 \) for the output layer is \( 1 \times 5 \):
\[
b_2 = \begin{bmatrix}
b_{21} & b_{22} & \dots & b_{25}
\end{bmatrix}
\]

The output of the network \( Y \) is calculated as:
\[
Y = H W_2 + b_2 = \begin{bmatrix}
y_1 & y_2 & \dots & y_5
\end{bmatrix}
\]

### Final Output

The final output vector \( Y \) is the result of applying the activation function to the linear combination of the hidden layer outputs and the weights and biases of the output layer:
\[
Y = \text{Activation}(H W_2 + b_2)
\]
